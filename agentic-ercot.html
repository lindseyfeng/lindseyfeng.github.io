Agentic ERCOT
An LLM agent trading the Texas (ERCOT) electricity market at HB_NORTH, updated daily.
The DART Trading agent is a prior-guided virtual bidder: it starts from an always-short prior, lets the LLM propose departures that a statistical prior must corroborate, and holds 1 MW on every traded hour. Positions are fixed at bid close, 10:00 CT the day before, and scored against the realized RT−DAM spread.
