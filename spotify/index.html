The story

A playlist is a person describing their own taste in their own words. We follow a million of them from what they are called, to which songs they hold, to the listener behind them.

Why it matters

Recommendations usually start from what you played. Playlists also say why: for a workout, a drive, a bad day. Yet — of playlists have two followers or fewer, so this signal is personal and mostly unseen.
